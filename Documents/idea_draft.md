**App name:** [HomeworkPal]

## 1. Background

- **Who would use this app?** [College/High School Students]
- **What problem do they want to solve?** [Students can easliy lose track of their homework/project due dates as well as their extracircular activities.]

## 2. Significance

- **Why does it matter?** [Students need a clean and easy way to track homework. This is a simple to use checklist app that can also work with your apple calendar.]
- **Would users pay for it? How much?** [No. This is free. Hence why people would use it.]

## 3. Innovation

- **What makes it different from competing apps?** [It's free, open source, designed for apple, and private]
- **Unique features / selling points:**
  - [Clean, easy to use modern UI]
  - [Can add homework due dates to calendar]
  - [Data is not shared and kept local, with the option to back it up to iCloud]
